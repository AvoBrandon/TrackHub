# Sources for the rules (checked Nov 13 2026)

Return windows
* Amazon 2026 holiday: items bought Nov 1–Dec 31 returnable through Jan 31 2027; Apple-brand through Jan 15; Amazon says this applies every year. https://www.valueaddedresource.net/amazon-2026-holiday-returns/ , https://ppc.land/amazon-makes-its-january-31-holiday-return-deadline-an-annual-rule/
* Best Buy 2026 holiday: purchases Oct 27–Dec 31 returnable through Jan 14 2027; excludes activatable devices and major appliances; standard 15 days / 60 for Plus & Total members. https://returnpolicyinfo.com/best-buy-holiday-return-policy/ , https://www.parcelmonitor.com/blog/holiday-return-policy-2026 (verify against bestbuy.com/returns before relying on a borderline date)

Price adjustment / card protection
* Best Buy Price Match Guarantee covers its own price drops during the return window. https://www.bestbuy.com/site/help-topics/price-match-guarantee/pcmcat297300050000.c
* Card price protection status 2026: https://upgradedpoints.com/credit-cards/credit-card-price-protection-policies/ , https://financebuzz.com/credit-cards-with-price-protection , https://www.usersrated.com/credit-cards-price-protection

Flights
* US DOT automatic refund rule (14 CFR Part 260; 3h/6h significant delay) and 2025–2027 enforcement pauses: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-07-07/html/2026-13675.htm , https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-12-05/html/2025-22140.htm
* EU261 amounts (250/400/600, halving rule), UK261 (220/350/520), Canada APPR (400/700/1000): regulation texts (EC 261/2004, UK retained version, SOR/2019-150).
